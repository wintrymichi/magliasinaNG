switzerlandNG

by wintrymichi

BeamNG.drive levels of any part of Switzerland, from open data

v__VERSION__

1 Pick an area

    Drag on the map to draw the area you want to drive in (up to 8.4 km a side). Move it or its corners afterwards. The dashed square is the terrain the level gets around it (6.1 km for an area up to 2.1 km a side, 12.3 km for a bigger one).

    2 Build it

    Everything is automatic: the open data is downloaded (swisstopo, cadastral survey, OpenStreetMap, the Register of Buildings), the level is built with the magliasinaNG pipeline and put in BeamNG's mods folder. It takes a few hours; you can close the window and resume later.
