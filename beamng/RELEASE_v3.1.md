**MagliasinaNG v3.1** for BeamNG.drive 0.40. First disable any other magliaso_pura zip in the mods folder (it is the same level).

**In-game test** (24 October 2026, BeamNG.drive 0.40.3, Windows, Direct3D 12, RTX 4070, 2048 x 1152, Ultra preset): loads in about **229 s**, peak **16.8 GB of RAM** (about 12.5 GB once loaded) and **7.2 GB of video memory**, **54-78 FPS** at six places. The screenshots below are from that test.

**The AI traffic is still broken** ([#83](https://github.com/wintrymichi/magliasinaNG/issues/83)): the cars queue behind the player's car and crash at the traffic islands.

## What is new
- **Magliasina bridge at the Magliaso roundabout:** one 25 m deck taken from the survey and the LiDAR. The river no longer shows through it.
- **Bridges as wide as the road:** about 20 bridges were bottlenecks. They now take the width of the bridge polygon, or of the road at their ends, and Ponte Tresa is 11 m. The steps between deck and road at Magliaso are gone.
- **Via Golf underpass under the FLP** (Caslano - Magliaso): the road now runs under the tracks instead of ending against the embankment.
- **No floating roofs** (469 -> 0): canopies, covered car parks and platform roofs have posts or walls, and about 4,300 posts stand under porches and lean-tos.
- **Detailed roofs:** a rim with tile heads, fascias, ridge caps, and new coppi, tegole and metal textures.
- **Cut slopes along the roads:** rock down to the asphalt, less green earth, and 90,000 bushes on the steep faces.
- **Wilder undergrowth:** ferns, shrubs, deadwood and fallen trunks, denser at wood edges and in clearings.
- **Railway:** the ballast reaches up to the sleepers, and the rails no longer climb into the wall between Caslano and Ponte Tresa.
- **Walls:** fewer saw-tooth tops (some walls of the Magliaso - Pura Cantonale, the hairpin among them, still have them in the game).
- **Street lamps at night:** downward cones as in Italy, so the road is lit between the lamps.
- **Time trials:** the cars start facing the right way.
- **Pavements on kerbs:** 12 cm kerbs, lowered at entrances and pedestrian crossings.
- **Roundabouts:** a raised green middle on its kerb inside a mountable ring of setts.
- **Less uniform asphalt:** newer and older stretches, patches and joints read from the 2024 orthophoto, cracks only where the road is old.
- **19 buildings closer to the real ones** along the main roads (plaster colours and details from open street photos).
- **Forest floor as a mosaic:** ragged wood edges, thickets on sunny slopes, bare leaf litter under the beeches, rock outcrops, ferns and stones on the wooded banks.
- **Bus stops:** yellow zigzags, BUS letters and yellow lines as on the orthophoto, and crossings the rules had missed.
- **Traffic islands:** the keep-right poles have the black and yellow sleeve of the Swiss island posts.
- **Bus shelters** placed again: none on the carriageway any more (6 stood on it), squared to the road beside their stop.

## Known issues
- Via Golf underpass: the two footways beside the road under the tracks lie about 2 m above the road (a raised strip under the deck), and the deck is 2.9 m over the road at its north edge. The stone wing walls are out of the asphalt.
- The 8 marked crossings added at bus stops were laid after the pavements were raised, so their kerbs stay 12 cm high instead of being lowered; to be fixed in the next build.
- Magliaso bridges: a step of up to 0.4 m at the south-east corner of the main deck and 6-8 cm at the upstream bridge, both outside the lanes.
- The level is 7.9 GB unpacked (+1.3 GB, mostly the cut slopes). Expect a lower frame rate in the woods.
- AI traffic still broken (#83): 13 cars queued behind the player's car on the Magliaso bridge, then ran into the traffic island at the roundabout exit.
- Seen in the game test: saw-tooth wall tops still on the Magliaso - Pura Cantonale; the Via Golf underpass deck is an untextured grey slab; the island posts miss two materials; grass on the asphalt of the Ponte Tresa bridge at its Italian end.
- The time trials were not driven in the game test. Please report anything odd in the issues.
