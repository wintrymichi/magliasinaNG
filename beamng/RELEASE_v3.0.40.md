BeamNG.drive map of the **Malcantone**, version 3.0.40: [v3.0](https://github.com/wintrymichi/magliasinaNG/releases/tag/v3.0) made for **BeamNG.drive 0.40**, with the fixes and additions since v3.0. On 0.40, v3.0 shows missing textures on the stone walls and white or grey squares among the far trees; v3.0.40 has neither. Caslano and Arosio are now whole, the churches and bell towers look like themselves, the Monte Lema cable car runs up from Miglieglia, and there are four time trials.

## Made for BeamNG.drive 0.40

0.40 moved 73 of the game's files the map uses (mostly the textures of Italy) to new folders, and three of them left nothing at the old place: the stone walls and some plastic parts showed missing textures. The map points at the new places. 192 textures that were identical copies (the outlines of the bus stop signs, the direction signs of one shape) are now one file each, so the game converts and keeps fewer of them at every start.

## No squares among the far trees

On 0.40 the far trees on the slopes turned into opaque white or grey squares (from one tree in ten to a whole hillside) whenever the game made their pictures for the distance itself during the session, which it does at the first load of every new version of the map. The far trees now ship those pictures ready-made beside their models, as the game's own trees do, and the game no longer makes them.

## Road wear without light patches or holes

The tyre tracks, patches and cracked stretches take the shadows of cars, trees and walls (they stayed very light under them), go on without breaks through junctions, lay-bys and bridges (tyre tracks 270 → 325 km), and the bare road no longer shows through them (from 1–5 % of their area to 0.06 %).

## Caslano and Arosio whole

The whole municipality of Caslano on land, with the golf course, the lakeside to the Torrazza and the whole Monte Caslano, and the whole village of Arosio: the map grows from 56.0 to 58.6 km² (issue #16).

## Churches, bell towers and the Monte Lema cable car

Bell chambers with their bells under a cornice, clock faces lit at night on the tall towers, crosses, portals, rose windows, arched windows, granite plinths and stone quoins, also on round towers (Novaggio), towers with a lantern (Agno) and towers inside a village block (Bedigliora); stone churches where the photos show bare stone (Novaggio, Santo Stefano in Miglieglia, San Michele in Arosio, Mugena) (issue #11). The cable car from Miglieglia has its ropes, lattice pylons and blue and green cabins, and the summit its white radar dome and mast.

## Four time trials

Missions of the game with checkpoints and its leaderboard, started from the big map or at their start on the road: the hillclimbs Magliaso – Pura (3.7 km, +238 m) and the Penudria Gravesano – Arosio (4.1 km, +491 m), the hairpins Astano – Banco (3.0 km) and the village stage Novaggio – Miglieglia – Breno (5.6 km), each also the other way round. Up to v3.0 the map was free roam only.

## Also

- **Traffic by the Swiss rules:** the AI roads are welded at the junctions (235 of 242 km in one network), Swiss speed limits from 20 to 80 km/h, a lane each way on two-way roads from 4.5 m, STOP and give way, European cars; where a footpath area meets a road, the road keeps its height to the edge of the carriageway (no more steps of 1–2.5 m in the lane, e.g. on Via Cantonale). See *Known* for the traffic itself.
- **The sound of water:** the game's own water sounds by the rivers (Magliasina, Tresa, Vedeggio), the streams where a road crosses them and the waves of the lake shore, 174 places.
- **Walls without holes** (issue #76): the roadside walls of the cantonal road are closed at their ends and at the back, their feet reach under the asphalt and the steep ground, and the tops of wall pieces of different heights are no longer pulled down where they meet.
- **Fewer steps on the roads:** the big step after the hairpin below Astano towards Banco, the steps on narrow roads and junctions at Vezio and Arosio, the bridge heads; no more trees standing in the road at Vezio.
- **Direction signs at every junction with a main road**, pointing to the villages each road reaches first.
- **Hedges and fountains from open street photos** (Mapillary, Panoramax, KartaView; no Google imagery): hedges where the photos see a hedge and none where they see a wall or a fence, and the village fountains as granite basins with their pillar and spout.
- **In English in the game's level list**, shorter, with the aerial shot over Novaggio as the preview.
