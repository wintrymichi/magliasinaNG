# Changelog

What changed in each version of the map, newest first. The release notes that go with each zip are in [`beamng/`](beamng) (`RELEASE_vX.Y.md`); the technical notes for each version (which script does what) are in [`beamng/README.md`](beamng/README.md).

## Next version (v3.1, in preparation)

Already in the code after the v3.0.40 zip (nightly #2), in the next build:

- **Lids over the grooves between two walls** (`walls.wall_gaps_step`): where the survey draws one wall as two lines that were not joined, or a wall has a notch at a pillar, two walls stood a few centimetres apart and showed a dark groove from above (1,100 gaps, 8.6 km in v3.0 and v3.0.40): every gap narrower than 25 cm between the wall tops gets a lid in the material of the tops beside it, and a face down into the ground where it opens at the end of the walls. The hole check of `check_level` no longer counts slivers, faces back to back and T joints as holes.
- **No road under the lake at the Caslano slipway** (`network.LakeCut`): the surveyed carriageway polygon of the slipway went on into the lake (30 road faces under the water in v3.0.40); the surveyed paved polygons and road strips now stop at the shore, cut only where the ground as low as the lake touches the surveyed lake (the floor of the Tresa valley below Ponte Tresa is as low, and a first version cut away its yards and roads).
- **Arosio's north edge in the OpenStreetMap extracts** (`download_osm.py --add`, from the Geofabrik extract): the roads and places of the strip of Arosio the extract did not cover.
- **The far trees' imposters come from the build** (`far_trees.prebaked`): v3.0.40 got them by a patch of its zip; `check_level` counts far models without them (`far_models_without_imposters`).
- **Street lamps that light the road at night** (`props.lamp_light`, `lamps.lamps_step`): every lamp had a PointLight of 14 m radius under its head, 8.4 m up: it lit a small pool under the lamp and left the 30 m between two lamps dark. Now a SpotLight aimed down, as the game's Italy level lights its road lamps (range 40 m, cone 120/165°, 6000 lm, warm 3845 K, calibrated in the game, still without shadows and on only at night); the lights already in the zip the step reads are rebuilt the same way.

## v3.0.40 (20 October 2026)

For BeamNG.drive 0.40, with the fixes after the v3.0 release. The zip is the nightly #2 of the integration branch (built from `afad5c1`), with the far trees' imposters added afterwards (`2bc800f`, `nightly_patch.yml`), tested in the game on 20 October 2026.

- **Textures in BeamNG 0.40** (`game_paths.py`, `dati/game_paths_0.40.json`): 0.40 moved 73 of the 367 files of the game the map uses from the levels' folders to `/assets/materials/`; three of them left nothing behind at the old place (the colour and normal maps of Italy's medieval stone wall, `italy_plastic_d`) and showed as missing textures. The map points at the new places (checked in an installed 0.40.0.0). The glazing of the bus shelters has its material (`Italy_bld_ind_windows`, which the level never defined).
- **No white squares among the trees** (`far_trees.py`, `dati/far_imposters`): BeamNG 0.40 drew the far trees on the slopes as opaque white or grey squares (from one in ten to the whole hillside, until the game was closed) when it made their pictures for the distance itself, during the session: at the first load of every new version of the map. The far trees now ship those pictures ready-made beside their models, as the game's own trees do, and the game no longer makes them (checked in the game: no squares, nothing baked).
- **Fewer textures to load** (`same_textures.py`): 192 of the level's 654 texture files were identical copies (the outline of the 112 bus stop signs, the direction and street name signs of one shape); each is now one file the materials share. BeamNG 0.40 converts the level's PNG textures again at every start, and a laptop with 512 MB of video memory ran out of it while converting the bus stop signs of v3.0.
- **Road wear takes the shadows** (`italy_mats.OVERLAY`): the tyre tracks, patches, cracked stretches and dusty edges are drawn with Italy's flags for the same textures (translucent with depth writes). Without them the game drew them in a pass without shadows: the cracked stretches stayed very light under cars, trees and walls. Checked in the game on the same patch (car shadow: luma 103 against 203 of the lit patch; before 190 against 203).
- **Road wear without breaks** (`road_wear.py`): the AI roads that go on as one another are joined before the wear is laid (1,996 points: bridges, changes of name, the road going straight on through a T or a crossing), and the bands go on past a jump of the width up to 40 m long (lay-bys, the mouths of side roads), and the road going on keeps its tracks where a side road reaches into it. Tyre tracks 270 → 325 km.
- **Road wear without holes** (`road_wear.py`): the crown of the road, its lane joints and crests came up through the bands of wear over 1-5 % of their area (the bare road showing through the tracks and patches). The bands now have rows of vertices at most 2 m apart across them and are lifted wherever the asphalt comes too close (0.04-0.06 % left, only at real steps of the road surface), and a band no longer hangs over a step like a sheet.
- **Ambient sounds** (`ambient_sounds.py`, `dati/ambient_events.json`): the game's own water sounds near the roads: the rivers (Magliasina, Tresa, Vedeggio), the streams where a road crosses them (quieter) and the lake shore (waves), 174 emitters (75 on the rivers, 50 on the streams, 49 on the lake shore). BeamNG 0.40 has no church bells, birds or crickets; the forest ambience of the game can be switched on for the woods in `ambient_events.json` ("use") once heard in the game.
- **Traffic and AI drivers** (`ai_roads.py`, `markings_rules.py`): the AI roads are welded at the junctions (43 → 33 separate pieces, 235 of 242 km in one network), their widths have no jumps, Swiss speed limits from 20 to 80 km/h, a lane each way on two-way roads from 4.5 m, cut-off pieces closed to traffic; stop signs in `signals.json`, and before each give-way point a short piece of road the AI treats as minor, so it gives way (the game's AI has no give-way signal of its own); Swiss road rules and European traffic in `info.json`; where a footpath area meets a road, the road surface keeps the road's height up to the edge of its carriageway, so 1-2.5 m steps no longer cut into the lane (e.g. Via Cantonale).
- **Steps and trees on the roads** (`network_mesh.py`, `far_trees.py`, `vegetation.py`): the steps on narrow roads and at junctions (Vezio, Arosio), the big step after the hairpin below Astano towards Banco, bridge heads; far trees of the forest fill keep clear of the roads (trees standing in the road at Vezio).
- **Walls without holes** (`walls.py`, issue #76): the roadside walls of the cantonal road are closed at their ends and at the back (one saw under their top into the bank where they stop), wall feet reach under the asphalt and under steep ground, and the tops of wall pieces of different heights are no longer pulled down to the ground where they meet; `check_level` counts the holes left (`wall_holes`).
- **Direction signs at the junctions** (`direction_signs.py`): every junction with a main road gets the Swiss pointers to the villages each turning road reaches first, before the junction and on the side they point to.
- **Level info in English** (`build_level.write_info`): the title, description and details shown in the game's level list are in English and shorter, and the level's preview is the aerial shot over Novaggio at the top of the README.
- **Recognisable churches, bell towers and the Monte Lema cable car** (`landmarks.py`, `landmark_textures.py`, `dati/landmarks.json`, issue #11): the churches, chapels and bell towers of the survey get a bell chamber with bells under a cornice, clock faces lit at night on the tall towers, a cross on the spire and the front gable, a portal, a rose window, arched windows, a granite plinth and stone quoins, also on round towers (Novaggio), towers with a lantern (Agno) and towers the survey keeps inside the church or a village block (Bedigliora). The churches with bare stone walls in the reference photos are stone (Novaggio, Santo Stefano in Miglieglia, San Michele in Arosio, Mugena). The cable car Miglieglia - Monte Lema (swissTLM3D) has its ropes, lattice pylons and blue and green cabins, and the summit its white radar dome and mast.
- **Caslano and Arosio whole** (`paesi_interi.py`, `dati/paesi_interi.json`, issue #16): up to v3.0 both villages were in the map only within 100 m of the roads added in v2.2 and v2.8. Now the whole municipality of Caslano on land (the village east of the cantonal road with the golf course, the lakeside to the Torrazza and the whole Monte Caslano, with 30 m of lake along the shore) and the whole village of Arosio (its buildings in the federal register, GWR) are in the area: 56.0 → 58.6 km². The register extract kept in the repository only grows by the new buildings (`download_gwr.py --add`).
- **Time trials** (`races.py`): four races with checkpoints and the game's own leaderboard, as timeTrial missions of the game (start on the map and the big map, gates every 300 m, each also the other way round): the hillclimb Magliaso - Pura on the cantonal road (3.7 km, +238 m), the hillclimb of the Penudria Gravesano - Arosio (4.1 km, +491 m), the hairpins Astano - Banco (3.0 km) and the village stage Novaggio - Miglieglia - Breno (5.6 km). They follow the AI roads of the level as built. Up to v3.0 the map was free roam only.
- **Hedges, fountains and roofs from open street photos** (`street_*.py`, `street_reference.yml`, `dati/street_hedges.json`, `dati/street_fountains.json`): the open street-level photos (Mapillary, Panoramax, KartaView; no Google imagery) are fitted to the lidar model of the area and segmented. Where they see a low strip of the surface model along a street they tell a hedge from a wall or a fence: hedges are planted where they see vegetation and dropped where they see a wall. The fountains and drinking water points of OpenStreetMap are a granite basin with its pillar and spout (a small bowl for the drinking taps), sized from the photos where they show it. Benches, bins, hydrants and junction boxes the photos see where OpenStreetMap has none are added (`dati/street_objects.json`). The photos were analysed over the whole area. No roof is moved (`dati/street_roofs.json` stays empty): in the three test zones the roof lines agreed with the game's within about 0.5 m, and the 31 roofs the whole area found 0.5-2.9 m off, almost all too low in the game, look like a bias of the measurement until they are checked (`verifica/street/roofs_measured.json`). Windows and doors measured from the photos are not used yet (`MAGLIASO_STREET_FACADES=1`): the detector misses about half of them.

Built and checked automatically on GitHub Actions (`v3 build test`: zip 542 MB, no sign or guard rail drawn twice); the in-game test is still to do. Release notes: [`beamng/RELEASE_v3.0.md`](beamng/RELEASE_v3.0.md).

## v2.8 (10 October 2026)

- **Tested in the game:** the released zip (the *final build test* workflow's) in BeamNG.drive 0.39, native Linux build, RTX 4070: loads in 130 s, 6.4 GB of memory at its peak, no crash. Guard rails whole, roundabout signs, the road Arosio – Mugena – Vezio – Breno – Miglieglia – Novaggio drivable end to end (9.6 km), the underside of the Magliaso bridge clean ([`beamng/verifica/v2.8/ingame_test_report.md`](beamng/verifica/v2.8/ingame_test_report.md), screenshots in [`beamng/verifica/screenshots/v2.8/`](beamng/verifica/screenshots/v2.8)).
- **Fixed after the first tests:** the large triangles of the terrain across the map; guard rails standing as posts only; signs, guard rails and wall faces drawn twice (guard rails 0 of 4,304 doubled, signs 1 of 591, wall faces 2,692 m² instead of 13,800 m²: `doubles.py`); the chequered mesh under the Magliaso bridge by the roundabout.
- **Known, still to fix:** pale jagged bands on some stone wall caps, pale slabs on the Magliasina bridge abutments, pale patches in the grass at Agno, one "keep right" sign twice, the AI driver stopping at a few spots (Mugena, Vezio, Miglieglia, Pura). The AI drives are a diagnostic: a stop marks a spot to look at, not a road a player cannot drive.

- **Road markings laid from standard pieces along the road.** Up to v2.7 the paint of the network followed the orthophoto's tracing (cleaned in v2.7): lines wobbled against the road, a line traced twice ran as two staggered lines, crossings and bars took the angle of their traced blobs, and arrows and letters were the traced blobs. Now (`markings_std.py`) the orthophoto only says what is painted where; the paint is laid from a dataset of the Swiss markings (`beamng/dati/road_marking_templates.json`: line widths, dash lengths and periods, the yellow crossing, bar widths, six direction arrows) along the axis of the AI roads: lines parallel to the road on its lane lines (the lanes of OpenStreetMap, or the count that explains the traced lines best) or at one inset from its edge; dashed stretches at one dash, period and phase; crossings square to the road across the carriageway; stop and give-way lines square to the road; hatched areas at one angle and spacing; arrows the dataset's arrow that covers the traced blob, in the middle of its lane. Letters, fragments and paint at no lane line or edge are left out (on the v2.8 level: 943 lines, 237 dashed stretches, 82 crossings, 174 stop and give-way lines, 10 hatched areas, 61 arrows; 2,757 fragments and 895 blobs left out). The cantonal road's own paint, checked in the panoramas, stays as it was.
- **Houses no longer see-through from one side.** The game draws a face from its front only, and up to v2.7 the walls the ray test of v2.4 turned the wrong way (open shells of the survey, rows of houses, blocks; about 5 % of the wall area measured in the v2.8 build test) left those houses see-through from that side. Now the walls, plinths and photo facades are drawn from both sides by their materials (`buildings_mesh.WALL_TWO_SIDED`, the back face lit like the front), and the extra back-face copy of the undecided walls is gone: fewer triangles than before.
- **The ground behind the retaining walls shaded like the terrain.** Behind every retaining wall the terrain is lowered (a 1.5 m grid cannot hold a step inside a 0.3 m wall) and a mesh puts the ground back at its height: 63 ha in all. Up to v2.7 every triangle of that mesh was lit on its own, so the slopes behind the walls showed flat facets and saw teeth, lighter and darker, beside the smoothly shaded terrain (for example along the walls at Ponte Tresa, Agno and below the cantonal road to Pura). Now (`walls.wall_fill_step`) the mesh has the normals of the ground it restores, computed like the terrain's own, and the terrain's own normal where it meets the terrain: the same shapes, lit smoothly. Same geometry and collision as before.
- **No grass through the ground behind the walls, nor over low walls.** The game's grass grew on the terrain lowered under that mesh and stood through it, and on the foot of the walls lower than the grass it stood over their top. There the terrain keeps its material without the game's grass (200,441 terrain vertices, 0.3 % of the terrain); at the foot of the taller walls and on the meadows the grass stays.
- Checked so far with the 3D renderer of the pipeline (before and after at the same views: `beamng/verifica/v2.8/wall_fill/`); then in the game test of the release (see the top of this version).
- **Street lamps in the villages.** Up to v2.7 only the Magliaso–Pura cantonal road had street lamps (68, where the panoramas show them, and 5 more elsewhere). Now (`lamps.lamps_step`) 1,764 more stand along the roads of the villages, about one every 30 m on one side of the road, with the same model: where at least six buildings of the Federal Register stand within 45 m, on the pavement or the verge beside the carriageway, never on a carriageway, a wall, a bridge, the railway or in a building nor within 0.5 m of a roof (its gutter), clear of junctions, signs, furniture and trees. A plausible rule, not the real places: there are no open data on street lamps. In the old towns, where the lamps hang on the walls, there are few.
- **Every street lamp lights at night.** The lights under the lamp heads described for v2.4 never reached a release: the release builds take the cantonal road's objects from the v1.1 zip, older than the lights. Now every lamp, the 73 of v2.7 and the new ones in the villages, has its light: off by day, lit by the game after sunset, without shadows (`props.lamp_light`).
- Street lamps checked so far with the 3D renderer (where they stand: `beamng/verifica/v2.8/lamps/`); memory checked in the game test of the release; the lights at night not yet checked on their own in the game.
- **Darker ground in the woods.** The forest floor had the colour measured in the panoramas on the sunlit forest edges along the cantonal road, a light brown that showed between the trunks and, from afar, between the crowns. Now (`understory.understory_step`) it is a darker olive, the leaf litter in the shade (62, 66, 42 instead of 102, 98, 67).
- **Delineators and wooden pole lines between the villages.** Up to v2.7 the roads between the villages had no posts at all. Now (`poles.roadside_step`) white delineator posts with the black band stand on both sides of the main roads (6 m and wider) outside the villages, every 50 m on the straight and closer in the bends (499), except where a guard rail, a wall or a building already marks the edge; and lines of the game's wooden poles, one every 45 m with a cable between them, follow the country roads past the scattered houses (543 poles in 110 lines; a line ends where its cable would pass a street lamp or a house). A rule, not their real places: there are no open data on them.
- **The railway has its overhead line** (issue #21). The FLP (and the few electrified standard-gauge tracks of the Vedeggio valley) ran without its catenary. Now (`railway.catenary_step`) steel masts stand beside the tracks every 50 m (closer in the bends), on the outside of the bends, with a cantilever, a messenger wire, droppers and the contact wire 5.5 m over the rails, staggered from mast to mast: 168 masts over 8.1 km of track, where OpenStreetMap marks the track electrified. The masts are not in the open data either: a rule, at the tracks as the map has them. The masts stop a car, the wires do not.
- **Undergrowth.** Low shrubs (0.6–1.5 m, the game's own bush models the map already uses) grow on the forest floor around the camera, within 60 m, never on roads, paths, walls, buildings or the railway. The game places them as it goes: nothing is stored per shrub and they have no collision. Not yet checked on its own in the game.
- **Gutters, downpipes and things on the roofs.** Up to v2.7 the houses had their windows, doors and balconies drawn on the façades, but nothing on the outside of the walls and roofs. Now (`buildings_mesh.house_details_step`) every pitched roof has its gutters along the eaves, in zinc or copper, and downpipes down the corners; and on the houses, by a rule: TV aerials on a share of the older ones, satellite dishes turned to the satellites, and solar panels on some roofs facing south (305 km of gutters, 38,405 downpipes, 1,903 aerials, 1,483 dishes, 398 roofs with panels). Not where they really are: no open data has them. Checked so far with the 3D renderer (`beamng/verifica/v2.8/houses/`); not yet checked on its own in the game.
- **Piers and boats on the lake.** Up to v2.7 the lake shore had no pier at all. Now (`water.lake_step`) the 23 piers OpenStreetMap maps on the Swiss shore stand over the water (11 at Caslano, 4 at Magliaso, 4 at Agno, 3 at Ponte Tresa, 1 at Collina d'Oro): wooden decks on posts, or floating pontoons with their guide piles, 954 m in all. At the piers where boats moor, 137 boats lie bow to the pier: motor boats under their covers or open with an outboard, and sailing boats with their mast. The boats are drawn by the pipeline (low-poly, plain colours), not the real ones. Checked so far with the 3D renderer (`beamng/verifica/v2.8/lake/`); not yet checked on its own in the game.
- **Warning and parking signs where nobody mapped them** (issue #21). v2.7 put up the signs OpenStreetMap maps and those its rules imply; now (`signs_more.signs_more_step`) also, by the Swiss rules: a curve warning 150 m before the sharp curves of the main roads outside the villages that come after a straight (a double curve with the length of the winding stretch where several follow), the warnings of the level crossings (with or without barriers), falling rocks where OSM records them, and the blue P at the entrance of the public car parks: 80 signs, drawn after the Swiss standard like the others. Not every bend of a mountain road gets one, only the unexpected ones. Checked so far with the 3D renderer (`beamng/verifica/v2.8/signs/`); not yet checked on its own in the game.
- **Asphalt flush with the ground where nothing marks its edge.** Up to v2.7 the paved roads, pavements and yards stood on the ground like a slab, with a trench beside them: their edge 21 cm over the ground beside it (median; 46 cm on one edge in ten). The dirt tracks were laid flush in v2.7; now (`network_mesh.paved_edges_step`) the ground beside the paved surfaces is raised to 4 cm under their edge: 7 cm between asphalt and ground 30 cm from the edge (median). The asphalt itself does not move, so the roads drive exactly as before. Beside guard rails, fences, walls, houses, the railway and bridge parapets the step stays as it was. Checked so far with the 3D renderer (`beamng/verifica/v2.8/paved_edges/`); not yet checked on its own in the game.
- **Lit windows at night.** After sunset about a third of the windows whose glass shows, and most shop windows, glow in the warm light of a lit room (an emissive map on the glass that the game switches on at night: no light source, one 1024 × 512 texture).
- **Guard rails from the game.** The guard rails are the models of the game's Italy level, with their posts, end pieces and the game's own collision, one 3 m module after the other along the measured runs: the pipeline's guard rail meshes had broken collisions.
- **No more water triangles along the Magliasina.** The river water ran in and out of the banks of the torrents in triangles (a fifth of it under the ground, much of the rest a few centimetres over it): it is now cut where the terrain stands over it, on a finer mesh.
- **No more pale triangles behind the walls.** Behind every retaining wall the terrain used to be lowered to the foot of the wall (a 1.5 m terrain grid cannot hold a step inside a 0.3 m wall) and a mesh in the colours of the terrain put the ground back: 1.1 million triangles on 78 ha that showed in the game as flat, pale facets with stepped edges, holes and loose pieces. That mesh is gone: the terrain behind a wall now keeps the ground (`walls.carve_terrain`), lowered only on the low side of the wall, and rises across the wall within one terrain step, inside the wall and as a short bank of earth at its foot. Beside a road the road's own carve still keeps the terrain under the asphalt.
- **The road from Arosio to Miglieglia.** The pass above Gravesano ended in Arosio: the cantonal road on through Mugena, Vezio, Fescoggia and Breno down to Miglieglia and Novaggio left the map. Now it is in the map with a 100 m corridor, like the roads added in v2.2 (`strade_extra.py`, `beamng/dati/strade_extra_v22.json`), so from Arosio the round over the Alto Malcantone goes on to Novaggio and back down to the lake. The building register extract (`beamng/dati/gwr_area.json.gz`) is taken again over the wider area, for the houses of the new villages.
- **Built from scratch again.** Everything that came after v2.4 as a patch script on the previous zip (v2.5 to v2.8) is now a finishing step of the build itself (`build_level.py`, `build_level.FINISH`); the release workflow builds the level from the open data, as up to v2.4.

Fixed on the way: plates upside down, the game showing the old converted shapes, textures that did not load because their sides were not powers of two, dark rectangles around the panorama plates kept as measured.
- **Road markings redrawn as they are painted in Switzerland.** Up to v2.6 the markings of the network (208 km of roads outside the cantonal road) were the raw trace of the orthophoto: wobbling lines, dashes of different lengths, a line traced twice (the paint and the light concrete gutter beside it), solid lines broken wherever a car stood, pedestrian crossings made of a few yellow blobs, white blobs left by cars, glare and manholes. Now (`markings_clean.py`, into the zip with `patch_markings.py`):
  - lines follow the road smoothly (a quadratic fit over 14 m, which keeps the curves);
  - dashed lines have one dash length and one period per stretch, at the Swiss values where the measure is close (3 m dashes every 6 or 9 m in most places), with the dashes a car hid put back;
  - solid lines continue under parked cars and shadows (gaps up to 9 m) and under trees (up to 150 m, where the photo is hidden), never across a junction;
  - doubled lines and the gutter beside the edge line are gone;
  - 99 pedestrian crossings are standard Swiss ones (yellow bars 0.5 m wide and 0.5 m apart, across the whole carriageway, the lines along the road stopping 0.5 m before them), and 10 marked crossings of OpenStreetMap that the photo doesn't show (under a tree or a car) are added;
  - stop and give-way lines and the bars of hatched areas are clean rectangles; 1320 blobs that are neither a strip nor an arrow or letter are gone.
  - no paint on gravel and dirt: the light stones and puddles the orthophoto showed there (white pieces lying on the gravel above Arosio) are gone.
- The cantonal road Magliaso – Pura keeps its own markings (measured in the photos, state of October 2022).
- **Dirt and gravel roads and paths are part of the ground.** Up to v2.6 every unpaved track (36 ha of dirt and 10 ha of gravel roads, 19 ha of paths) stood on the terrain like a slab: the terrain under the road meshes was carved 0.1 m under the lowest face within 1.5 m, which left a trench on both sides, and the edge of the track showed about 0.3 m above the ground (median of the outer edges; 0.6 m at the 90th percentile). Now (`patch_unpaved.py`) the terrain around them is raised to their surface and fades back to the ground within 4.5 m, and their outer edges are lowered onto it: between track and ground there are 1–2 cm (median; under 5 cm on 93 % of the edges). The terrain stays under every road face; where a track runs on a wall or a bridge, and where it meets an asphalt road, nothing changes.
- **Wooded mountains again.** Since v2.3 the forest away from the roads was thinned to keep the game fluid: seen from the valley the slopes looked like a mountain with a few scraggy trees. Now every tree measured in the area is back (`far_trees.py`, into the zip with `patch_far_trees.py`): near the roads the same trees as before; farther away a lighter drawn tree (round or narrow broad-leaved, or a fir, in three shades from the orthophoto colour) that the game draws as a flat picture of itself beyond about ten metres, two triangles per tree.

## v2.6

- **New grass.** Up to v2.5 the grass was drawn by the pipeline in one texture without transparency maps: in the game it showed as dark opaque cards, and only up to 50 m. Now the meadows and gardens use the game's own grass and flower textures (colour, opacity, normal, roughness and ambient occlusion maps, light through the blades), laid out like the game's Italy level: short grass up to 50 m around the camera, short and long grass up to 120 m, meadow flowers (daisies, buttercups, geraniums, poppies), mown lawns in the gardens.
- Measured in the game: 72–111 fps at the test points on meadows, gardens and the cantonal road (v2.5: 82–128 at the spawn points).

## v2.5

The first version measured inside BeamNG.drive (0.39.4, on a PC with 16 GB of RAM, RTX 4070). The v2.4 level loaded in 215 s
(371 s through BeamMP) and then wanted more memory than the PC had: minutes on the loading screen and the whole PC lagging.
v2.5 has the same geometry, built so that the game needs much less:

- **7916 → 2188 objects:** the 128 m blocks of buildings, walls, road surfaces, guardrails, water and vineyards are merged 3 × 3.
- **38.2 → 19.7 million vertices:** the meshes were written with three own vertices per triangle; now the triangles share them (positions and texture coordinates unchanged, checked).
- **Road edges:** the skirts and kerb faces along straight stretches with fewer triangles, within 4 mm (15.9 → 15.3 million triangles).
- **Detail by distance:** guardrails are no longer drawn beyond about 600 m, fences 400 m, painted markings 500 m, walls 1.2 km, vineyards 1 km, buildings and roads 3 km (before: up to 12 km). Sun shadows up to 800 m instead of 1600.
- **Result in the game:** 60 s to load once the game has converted the shapes (about 145 s the first time), 82–128 fps at the spawn points.

The game still needs about 12 GB of memory at its peak: on a 16 GB PC close other programs (browser, Discord, ...) before playing.

Up to v2.3, 8.6 % of the wall area faced inwards (L- and U-shaped buildings, courtyard buildings, rows of houses): from some angles the houses were transparent, with floating roofs. Now the facing of each wall is decided with a ray test and inverted walls drop to 0.06 %; where the facing can't be decided the wall is double-sided, and there are soffits under the eaves.
- **Magliaso roundabout, the climb towards Pura.** The "cube" sticking out of the asphalt at the junction was the top of a retaining wall covered by the road, which the map raised by 16 cm; the "square" in the middle of the carriageway was the kerb of the traffic island removed in 2022. - **Dirt, gravel and paving where they really are:** the OpenStreetMap surface (otherwise swissTLM3D's) on every road and trail: the survey's roads, previously all asphalt, now have 36 ha of dirt, 9.4 ha of gravel and 1.2 ha of setts and cobblestones in the old towns. Textures drawn with the colour measured on the orthophoto; in the game the grip changes too.
- **Grass and flowers** on the meadows and in the gardens around the camera, **palms** (352) in the gardens near the lake, **rows** in 475 vineyards (465 km, in the direction seen in the orthophoto), **water in the rivers** (30 ha, under the bridges too).
- **Materials and light:** ambient occlusion for render, plinths and roofs, walls darker towards the ground, street lamp light at night, a little more haze over the lake.

## v2.3

- **Magliaso, the cantonal road towards Pura at the junction with Via Piscicoltura.** For about 60 m, right after the junction, the carriageway tilted towards the wall that separates it from Via Piscicoltura, lower down: at the edge it was up to 2.2 m below the real ground, with a dip as if the asphalt had collapsed. The surface computation took that wall for a bump and merged the two roads into a single surface. Now the wall is recognised and each road sits at its own height: on the axis of the cantonal road the largest deviation from the real ground goes from 1.70 m to 0.09 m, on Via Piscicoltura from 0.53 m to 0.24 m. The virtual drive test no longer finds any twists or lifted wheels there (in v2.2: twist 9.6, wheels lifted by 21 cm). The same fix improves another stretch of the cantonal road towards Pura (about 1.3 km from Magliaso); along the whole 3.7 km the points more than 35 cm from the real ground go from 110 to 72.
- **Lighter woods to draw.** Up to v2.2 every measured tree within 150 m of a road was in the map: slopes crossed by hairpins, like the pass above Gravesano seen from the village, were as dense as the real forest and the game slowed down when looking at them. Now all the trees within 30 m of roads and 5 m of trails remain; further away the forest is thinned keeping the tallest ones (up to 100 m from the road the tallest in every square 11–17 m across, beyond that in every 32 m square). In total 160,748 trees and shrubs instead of 239,753 (−33 %); on the slope of the pass above Gravesano 40 % fewer (from 6,165 to 3,722 trees within 700 m). Along the roads, in the first 30 m, the forest is the same as before.

## v2.2

- **New roads.** The pass above Gravesano to Arosio (Stradón da Rós, the «Penudria», with its hairpins), the Ponte Tresa – Caslano – Magliaso cantonal road and Via Torrazza in Caslano ended at the edge of the map: beyond, there was only terrain. Now they are real roads (7.2 km more) with a 100 m corridor of buildings, trees and walls, reviewed on Street View like the rest.
- **Buildings with façades.** Up to v2.1 every building was a rendered volume with no openings. Now the buildings have a total of 165,533 openings (windows with shutters, roller blinds or modern frames, doors, gates, garages, shop windows, church and barn windows), 5,915 balconies, plinths and 3,348 chimneys, laid out by floor and bay according to the use, period and number of floors in the Federal Register of Buildings. Roofs in curved tiles, flat tiles, stone slabs, sheet metal or flat roofs. All textures are drawn by the pipeline: no photos.
- **Old towns house by house.** swissBUILDINGS3D merges rows of houses into a single block; now every house of the official survey has its own façades, floors, door and colour (4,508 houses in blocks). Floors start from the pavement on the street front, on slopes too.
- **Colours measured in the photos.** The render colour of 3,637 buildings, and the shutter colour where it is clear, come from the Street View panoramas (numerical values only).
- **Shop windows** where OpenStreetMap records a shop, bar or office (173 buildings), **garages** with doors, stone **rustici**.
- **Missing buildings:** 538 buildings of the official survey built after the 3D survey have been added; 14 demolished ones removed.
- **Guardrails across the whole network.** 326 guardrail stretches seen in the Street View panoramas; 9.7 km built on the road edge in addition to the 2.0 km on the Magliaso–Pura cantonal road, which up to v2.1 were the only ones.
- **Walls** with original stone, concrete and render textures; the material comes from the photos where it can be seen clearly.
- **Bus stops** from OpenStreetMap with pole, sign, timetable and shelter.
- **More drivable roads.** The surfaces are continuous between carriageway, pavements and yards and at junctions: the step between two roads that meet is spread over a few metres, while the main carriageways stay as they are. On the same roads as v2.1 the virtual drive test counts 62 % fewer steps on minor roads (1695 → 646) and 20 % fewer on main roads (74 → 59); wheels lifting off the road drop by 56 % on minor roads and 58 % on main roads, hard hits by 24 % and sharp twists by 58 % on minor roads.
- **No obstacles in the passages** under buildings: windows and plinths no longer hang over the road.
